# Problem report workflow

1. The customer reports a problem through the portal or USSD, or staff log it for them. Status: **Open**.
2. A manager assigns it to a field officer, or it is routed by zone. Status: **Assigned**. The customer receives an SMS.
3. The field officer visits and fixes the problem.
4. The field officer records notes and an optional photo. Status: **Resolved**. The customer receives an SMS.
5. Any repair cost is recorded by the accountant as an expense.

## Exceptions to define

- The problem cannot be fixed on the first visit.
- The report is a duplicate of an existing report.
- The problem is the customer's responsibility, not the scheme's.
