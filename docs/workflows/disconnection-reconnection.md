# Disconnection and reconnection workflow

Applies only to organizations that enable a disconnection rule.

## Disconnection

1. An account becomes overdue beyond the organization's rule (days or amount).
2. It appears on the field action list for its zone.
3. A manager reviews and **approves** the disconnection, or leaves it.
4. The customer receives a warning SMS.
5. The field officer visits, disconnects, and records the meter reading and a photo.
6. The connection status becomes **Disconnected**. It is no longer billed or counted for the subscription.

## Reconnection

1. The customer pays the overdue amount and the reconnection fee by M-Pesa.
2. The account appears on the field officer's reconnection list.
3. The field officer reconnects and records the meter reading.
4. The connection status returns to **Active** and billing resumes from that reading.

## Exceptions to define

- The customer pays after approval but before the visit.
- The field officer cannot access the meter.
- A disconnected meter shows usage at the next visit (possible illegal reconnection).
