# Tenant move-in and move-out workflow

Applies to property managers. The meter belongs to the unit; tenants come and go.

## Move-in

1. The field officer selects the building and unit.
2. The field officer records the tenant's name and phone number.
3. A **move-in reading** and meter photo are recorded.
4. The tenant becomes the unit's account holder. Usage before the move-in reading stays with the previous account holder or the landlord.
5. The tenant receives an SMS verification code and can use the portal or USSD.

## Monthly

The unit's meter is read and billed to the tenant like any other connection. The tenant may never see the meter; every bill shows the previous reading, current reading, and units used, and the meter photo is visible in the portal.

## Move-out

1. The field officer records a **move-out reading** and photo.
2. The system produces a **final bill** on the tenant's account.
3. Any credit is applied to the final bill.
4. Any remaining unpaid amount stays on the tenant's account, not the unit.
5. Any remaining credit is refunded to the tenant by the accountant outside the system and recorded as a refund.
6. The unit becomes **empty**.

## Empty unit

While a unit is empty, its usage is **billed to the landlord**, until the next tenant's move-in reading.

## Exceptions to define

- The tenant leaves without notice and no move-out reading is taken.
- The move-out reading cannot be taken because the unit is locked.
- A tenant moves from one unit to another in the same building.
