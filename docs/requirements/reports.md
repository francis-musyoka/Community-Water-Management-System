# Reports

Reports are for **internal management** — managers, accountants, committees, and org admins. Reports for regulators such as WASREB are out of scope.

All reports can be generated for a selected scheme and period, and exported to **PDF** (for committee meetings) and **Excel** (for accountants). Organizations with several schemes can also view reports across all their schemes side by side.

## Financial reports

### Revenue and collections summary

Total billed, total collected through M-Pesa by revenue category, donor and government funding, and outstanding unpaid balances.

### Profit and loss statement

Revenue compared with operating costs by expense category.

### Unmatched payments

M-Pesa payments still waiting to be matched by the accountant, with age.

### Customer statement

One customer's bills, payments, credits, refunds, and balance over time. Used for disputes and tenant move-outs.

## Operational reports

### Monthly progress and water-loss report

Water produced at the source, water sold, active pumping days, and estimated water lost.

### Unread meters

Meters with no reading in the period and the reason given. Meters blank several periods in a row are highlighted.

### Field action list

Overdue accounts grouped by zone for follow-up, and accounts eligible for disconnection under the organization's rules.

### Disconnections and reconnections

Pending approvals, completed disconnections, reconnections, and reconnection fees collected.

### Problem reports

Open and resolved reports and average time to resolve, by zone.

## Compliance reports

### Customer compliance audit

Customers who have not verified their phone accounts or whose signed contracts have not been uploaded.

## Organization-type reports

### Property manager view

Totals by building and unit, including empty units billed to the landlord.

### Organization-wide view

For organizations with several schemes, such as counties: key figures for each scheme side by side.

## Requirements

- **RP-01:** Reports can be generated for a selected scheme and period.
- **RP-02:** Reports include the measures described above.
- **RP-03:** Reports can be exported to PDF and Excel.
- **RP-04:** Overdue accounts can be grouped by zone.
- **RP-05:** Verification and missing-contract exceptions can be identified.
- **RP-06:** Unread meters and their reasons are reported.
- **RP-07:** Unmatched payments are reported.
- **RP-08:** A statement can be produced for any customer.
- **RP-09:** Property managers can report by building and unit.
- **RP-10:** Multi-scheme organizations can compare schemes side by side.
- **RP-11:** Donor and government funding is shown separately from customer collections.
- **RP-12:** Committee members can view and export reports but not change data.
