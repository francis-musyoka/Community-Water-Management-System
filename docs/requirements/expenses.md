# Expenses and funding

## Purpose

The system records operating expenses and non-customer funding so each scheme can understand its running costs and prepare financial reports.

## Recording expenses

- **The accountant records expenses** against a scheme.
- Expenses are **approved by the committee in its meetings, outside the system**. The accountant records an expense after it is approved.
- Each expense has:
    - Date
    - Category
    - Amount
    - Quantity and unit where applicable (litres of diesel, kilograms of chlorine)
    - Payment method (M-Pesa, bank, or cheque)
    - Optional approval reference, such as the meeting date or minute number
    - Optional **receipt upload** (photo or PDF)
- A wrong expense is edited or cancelled, never deleted. Every change is logged.

## Expense categories

The system provides default categories. Each organization can **add its own custom categories**, for example bulk water bills for a property manager.

### Default categories

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Pumping and fuel | Diesel for pumps or generators, power units |
| Water treatment | Chlorine, soda ash, other chemicals |
| Maintenance and repairs | Pipe burst repairs, fittings, engine servicing, pump spare parts |
| Staff salaries | Payroll |
| Office costs | Rent, stationery, airtime, transport, meeting allowances, audit fees |

## Donor and government funding

The accountant records funding that does not come from customers, with source (donor, county, NGO, or other), amount, date, and purpose. Funding is reported separately from customer collections.

## Requirements

- **EX-01 — Record expenses:** The accountant records expenses against a scheme.
- **EX-02 — Categorize expenses:** Expenses use default or organization-defined categories.
- **EX-03 — Custom categories:** Org admins can add expense categories.
- **EX-04 — Quantities and costs:** Quantity and unit can be stored alongside cost.
- **EX-05 — Receipt upload:** A receipt photo or PDF can be attached.
- **EX-06 — Approval reference:** An optional committee approval reference can be recorded.
- **EX-07 — No deletion:** Expenses are edited or cancelled with an audit trail, not deleted.
- **EX-08 — Funding:** Donor and government funding can be recorded separately from customer income.
- **EX-09 — Reporting:** Expenses and funding are included in financial reports and the profit and loss statement.
